## Findings (written by hand after the run)

Run on 2026-11-11 with 49 tasks (10 English prompts for India on four series, a German and a Hindi prompt on three, 3 priority tasks). It is a small sample, not the 200 prompts of doc 01 section 12: it shows what the answers look like and what they cost, not rates to publish.

1. **All three surfaces work through DataForSEO and answered every task.** 49 of 49 tasks returned an answer, none failed, none was refused. The task paths `task_post`, `tasks_ready` and `task_get/advanced/{id}` are right for the ChatGPT scraper, the Gemini scraper and AI Mode.
2. **Prices match doc 07 section 7.1.** $0.0012 a standard task on all three, $0.0024 on the priority queue. AI Mode showed no depth surcharge at default settings (S-06 pass criterion met).
3. **Turnaround is minutes, not the 45 minutes of the pricing card.** Medians: ChatGPT 77 s (slowest 176 s), ChatGPT with search forced 123 s (238 s), ChatGPT priority 65 s (95 s), Gemini 231 s (261 s), AI Mode 264 s. The waits come from a 30 second `tasks_ready` poll, so they are upper bounds, and Gemini and AI Mode finished within one poll of each other.
4. **ChatGPT variant: use the one that answers as a user sees it (`chatgpt_web`).** Rule of doc 07 section 4.2: sources on at least 60% of commercial prompts. Result: 6 of 6, and the forced variant also 6 of 6. The one natural answer without sources was a definition question.
5. **What each surface returns:**
   - ChatGPT: `markdown`, `sources` (url, domain, title, snippet, source_name, publication_date), `search_results`, `fan_out_queries`, `brand_entities`, `check_url`, `model` (`gpt-6`). Items are `chat_gpt_text`, `chat_gpt_table`, `chat_gpt_images`, `chat_gpt_local_businesses`. Sources on 11 of 12, search results on 11 of 12, fan-out on 11 of 12, provider brand entities on 8 of 12.
   - Gemini: `markdown`, `sources` with the same fields (11 of 12), `model`. No search results, fan-out or brand entities, as doc 07 says.
   - AI Mode: one `ai_overview` item with `markdown` and `references` (domain, source, text, title, url) on 12 of 12, plus `check_url`. No top-level `markdown`, no `model`, no fan-out.
6. **Non-English works.** German and Hindi prompts got answers in the same language on all three surfaces.
7. **Two cleanups the normalizer needs.** Gemini reports the model with a non-breaking space in one answer (`3.5\xa0Flash-Lite`): normalize whitespace before comparing models for version boundaries. The ChatGPT location list holds 192 locations, not 215 (India 2356 and Germany 2276 are in it).
8. **Not answered by this run.** No answer held an ad item (`chat_gpt_ad`) or a paid AI Mode element, so whether ad text enters `markdown` is still open; the normalizer keeps the doc 07 section 5.4 rule and gets a fixture when one is seen. Replicate independence (S-05) was not run. A weekly run is the first real chance to see ads and refusals.
9. **The tracked site shows up in cited sources more than in the answer text.** Eventus Security appears somewhere in the payload (sources or search results included) in 16 of 49 answers and in the answer text of 12. Leaving out the 4 answers to the prompt that names it, that is 12 and 8 of the other 45. This is why Cited and Mentioned are separate metrics.
