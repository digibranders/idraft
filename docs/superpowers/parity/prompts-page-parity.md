## How to read this file

- Columns: ID, Behaviour, Exact strings (quoted from the mock), Mock source, Real-app status (`todo` until ported, then `done <commit>` or `differs <reason>`), Package, How to verify.
- Packages: `P-shell` (page frame, toolbar, tabs, table chrome), `P-metrics` (metric columns and cells, strip numbers), `P-popovers` (brands, cited pages, screenshot, menus, popovers, Add prompts dialog), `P-groups` (Groups tab, group drawer), `shared` (used by several packages or by other pages), `e2e` (needs a browser test).
- Strings are copied from the mock. Since the glossary pass (`docs/glossary/final-list.md`, applied 2026-11-16) the mock and the real app use the same words: Platform, Group, Location, Prompts, Schedule (was Panel), Period (was window and date range), Brand (was Entity), Mention position (was Avg position), Cited pages (was Cited links), answer (was observation) and Google AI Mode and Google AI Overview in full. Section 1 lists the old and new words. Type and Branded stay.
- A string in backticks is verbatim. `${...}` marks a computed part. `U+2212` is the typographic minus used in negative deltas. The middle dot in "·" is a real character in the mock.
- Statistical rules R1 to R12 are the ones in `docs/ai-visibility/inventory/overview-and-charts.md` section 1.
- The mock has no loading state, no error state and no network. Rows that say "mock gap" describe something the real app must still decide.
