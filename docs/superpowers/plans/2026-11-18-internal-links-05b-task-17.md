## Decisions of this expansion

1. Row key is the API's `key` ("source>target"). Selection holds keys and belongs to the query: it is cleared when the status, tier, search, run or page changes and after a successful bulk decision.
2. One decision request runs at a time (a ref guard, buttons disabled while it runs). The list read is changed at once (optimistic): the row's status is set, a row that no longer matches the status filter leaves, `meta.counts` and `meta.total` follow. A failed request puts the saved list back and shows a toast with `reasonOf(error)` or a plain sentence (never the raw message). A settled request invalidates the internal links reads.
3. Planned fixes: `fetchApiBlob` is split out of `downloadApiFile` (`lib/api/download.ts`). The tab reads the CSV, and when it has no row after the header says "Nothing to export" without saving. The Export menu gets the filtered CSV through `registerExport` (same in-flight guard as the other tabs lives in the page's Export menu; the planned button has its own ref guard and a failure toast).
4. Focus after Accept, Dismiss and Undo: a ref holds the acted key and its index; an effect on the rows moves focus to that row's first decision button, else the button of the row now in that index, else the search box. After a bulk decision focus goes to the status chip in use.
5. Target tier options come from the workspace tiers (`context.tiers`) as an explicit list plus All and Untiered (`none`); never a generated enum.
6. The tab shows `LinksDropped` and makes no read when `crawl.linksAvailable` is false. A 410 from the read shows it too.
7. A viewer (no `projects:write`) sees rows, Copy and Planned fixes only.
