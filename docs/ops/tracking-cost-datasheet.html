How a cost is built

keywords × devices × checks per month × (depth ÷ 10 pages × page price + one page price for the AI Overview fee + one page price for the poisoned-results fix) prompts × checks per month × (3 engines × answers per engine × answer price) page price: $0.0006 standard · $0.0012 priority · $0.002 live; answer price: $0.0012 · $0.0024 · $0.004

Depth is how many results a check reads. DataForSEO bills each 10 results as one page: top 10 is 1 page ($0.0006), top 20 is 2 ($0.0012), top 30 is 3 ($0.0018), top 50 is 5 ($0.0030), top 75 rounds up to 8 ($0.0048) and top 100 is 10 ($0.0060). The AI Overview fee stays $0.0006 at every depth. The AI Overview fee is charged when Google shows an AI Overview that loads late, and refunded otherwise, so a real month lands between the two figures. The budget planner reserves the higher one. The poisoned-results fix is one more page price on every task, at any depth, and is charged whether or not poisoning is found, so it is not refunded.

Assumptions and limits

  • Poisoned-results fixGoogle sometimes returns pages to automated requests where real listings are replaced by others. In a test on 2026-11-23 the same 35 keywords found the Eventus site in 28 of 30 page-one runs with the fix, against 10 of 30 without it; at depth 75 it found it in 33 of 38 runs, against 21 of 38. The fix replaced listings on 61 of 70 tasks. It costs one more page price on every task (+$0.0006 standard): +10% at top 100, +23% at top 75 in the test, and +100% at top 10. The app does not send the parameter yet, so the figures with it on are planned.to build
  • Priority queueIt costs twice the standard price, and in our test (2026-11-23) it was about 10 times faster for depth-75 searches (85 seconds against 841) but only 20 to 35 percent faster for AI answers (65 to 73 seconds against 99 to 106). Its search results come back as Google redirect links (google.com/goto) instead of page addresses, so the ranking URL is lost and only the visible site address remains. Use the standard queue for scheduled checks.check
  • cloro costcloro bills credits, and credits come in monthly plans, so the figure shown is the price of the cheapest plan whose credits cover the month, with the plan name under it. A month that needs more than the Business plan's 2,800,000 credits goes to an enterprise contract. Credits do not roll over, and service stops at zero on every self-serve plan, so leave some headroom.
  • cloro scopecloro returns rankings, AI Overview sources and AI answers only. The monthly keyword data refresh (volume, CPC, difficulty, intent) has no cloro equivalent and stays on DataForSEO, so the calculator adds it to the cloro total. ChatGPT is priced with fan-out queries (7 credits) to match what DataForSEO returns by default.check
  • cloro in the testOn the same Eventus queries cloro matched Search Console about as well as DataForSEO does with its poisoned-results fix on, without needing a fix. The 2026-11-23 test is in the DataForSEO vs cloro report.
  • Billing questionsDataForSEO's pages settle the two open billing questions in our docs (Q-01): later pages cost full price, and the AI Overview fee is added once per task. Our own invoice has not yet been compared with these estimates.check invoice
  • DepthSince 2026-11-04 every check reads the top 100, so a rank shows as a number up to 100 or as ">100". A shallower check costs less (with the poisoned-results fix on: top 50 is 42% less, top 20 is 67% less, top 10 is 75% less; with it off: 45%, 73% and 82%), but a keyword ranking below the depth shows only as ">50", ">20" or ">10". The app offers only top 100 today, so a shallower depth would need a settings change.
  • Real checkEventus Security: 55 keywords × 2 devices, daily, is about $0.73 a day, or $21.78 a month at the planned rate without the fix. This matches the run cost seen on the server. With the poisoned-results fix it is about $0.79 a day, or $23.76 a month.
  • CalendarA month is 30 days for daily and alternate-day checks, and 4.33 weeks for weekly ones. Three times a week is 13 checks a month, twice a week is 8.66 and weekly is 4.33. Monthly is one check a month.
  • Lost-rank rechecksWhen a keyword drops out of the top 100, it is checked again up to 2 more times before the drop stands. This adds a few percent in a normal month and is not in the tables.
  • Keyword dataVolume and difficulty refresh monthly on the 20th: $0.012 per 700 keywords plus $0.00012 per keyword, so $0.02 for 100 keywords and $0.28 for 2,000. The calculator adds it.
  • QueueScheduled checks and Check now use the standard queue. The live queue costs $0.002 a page, 3.3× more.
  • Schedules todayThe scheduler runs daily, weekly and monthly. Alternate days, twice a week and three times a week need a schedule rule that is reserved in the schema but not built.to build
  • Prompt figuresAI prompt collection is designed (doc 07, section 7) but not built. The prompt figures use the verified DataForSEO task price of $0.0012.design
  • Doc 27The cost model doc still prices keywords at the old top 20 daily default ($0.057 a keyword a month). Since 2026-11-04 every check reads the top 100, so a daily keyword on one device is $0.18 to $0.198, or $0.198 to $0.216 with the poisoned-results fix.
