1. Use one word for one visible concept.
2. Prefer common nouns over product jargon.
3. Keep SEO and AI terms distinct where their data differs.
4. Name the user’s object, not the implementation job behind it.
5. Make labels work in singular, plural, filters, and sentences.
6. Reserve specialized terms for metrics that need precision.

3. Info button text

**AI Visibility**

What it shows: The share of valid answers that mention your brand, cite your website, or both.

How it is counted: Valid answers with a brand mention, a website citation, or both are divided by all valid answers; answers with neither are excluded from the numerator.

**Mention rate**

What it shows: The share of valid answers that mention your brand.

How it is counted: Valid answers that name your brand or an alias are divided by all valid answers; a brand can count once per answer.

**Citation rate**

What it shows: The share of valid answers that cite your website.

How it is counted: Valid answers with at least one citation to a configured website or page are divided by all valid answers; multiple citations in one answer count once.

**Share of voice**

What it shows: How much of the tracked-brand talk in answers is about your brand.

How it is counted: Your mentions are divided by all mentions of your brand and tracked competitors; adding or removing competitors changes every share.

**Average mention position**

What it shows: The average order in which your brand is named among tracked brands in answers.

How it is counted: The app records your brand’s first mention order in each answer where it appears, then averages those orders; lower is better.

**Sentiment**

What it shows: The tone of statements about your brand in valid answers.

How it is counted: Each answer is classified as positive, neutral, negative, or mixed for your brand; the displayed shares use valid answers with a sentiment classification.

**Search ranking position**

What it shows: Your organic position in search results for a tracked keyword.

How it is counted: The app records the position of the highest-ranking eligible page for the selected keyword, location, device, language, and check date; paid results and unsupported result types are left out.

**Detectable change**

What it shows: Whether a difference from the comparison period is large enough to distinguish from normal variation.

How it is counted: The app compares the current and comparison periods using its variation method; it labels a change only when the available data supports that conclusion.

4. Reasonable disagreements

| Choice | Options | My pick | Best argument against |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI product name | Platform, engine, provider | AI platform, shortened to Platform in AI screens | “Platform” is broad and can also mean a software platform; “engine” may feel more analytical. |
| Prompt organizer | Group, topic, cluster | Group | “Topic” better conveys subject matter and may be more natural for client reporting. |
| Configurable taxonomy UI | Classification, taxonomy, vocabulary | Classification | Existing SEO users may already understand “taxonomy,” and “classification” can sound formal. |
| Citation site name | Cited site, source, domain | Cited site | “Source” is conventional in AI reporting and more compact, if keyword provenance is renamed everywhere. |
| Generic monitored record | Brand, entity | Brand | Some monitored items are people, products, or organizations that are not brands. |

5. Wrong, missing, or unnamed concepts

- Google AI Overview should not be presented as a normal AI platform. It is a search-result feature and has a different collection method.
- “Overview” alone is unusable because it names two home pages and appears inside Google AI Overview.
- “Source” is overloaded twice: cited website and keyword acquisition origin. It should not remain visible for either without a qualifier.
- “Entity” is too abstract for normal UI, but the data model may need it if users can track people, products, or publishers rather than brands.
- “Panel” has no defensible user-facing meaning in this product. Rename it Prompt set.
- “Observation” should stay internal. It incorrectly merges fundamentally different records: an AI answer and a rank check.
- “Check now” is ambiguous if used outside SEO. Use “Run now” for collection actions, and “Rank check” for SEO measurements.
- “Country” and “Location” currently claim more precision than the AI product supports. AI screens should show Country until city-level collection exists.
- “Citation share” and “Source share” are not defined clearly enough. Decide whether each counts citation events, answers containing citations, distinct sites, or distinct pages before exposing either.
- “Recommendation” needs an explicit rule for what qualifies as a recommendation, especially for comparisons and negative recommendations.
- “Valid answer” needs a documented exclusion policy for failures, refusals, empty answers, blocked results, and malformed captures.
- “Branded” needs an alias-matching rule, including partial names and ambiguous names.
- “Detectable change” needs a visible link or expandable explanation of the method, confidence threshold, and minimum-data policy.
